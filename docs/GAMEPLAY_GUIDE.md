# Gameplay guide (Prologue + Chapter 1)

For parents, teachers and testers. Nothing can be lost: missing a jump or getting bounced just puts you back on safe ground, and the game saves at every checkpoint.

## Prologue (Director Script P00–P02)
1. **Title:** choose **Start Adventure** and pick the Girl or Boy Explorer. They have identical moves, powers and story.
2. **Bedroom:** watch the colors leave the drawing (press **Esc** to skip). Walk with **WASD/arrows**. Look at things with **E**. The **TEDDY** and **CRAYONS** are optional. The **WINDOW** is where Pip taps the glass. After 30 seconds, something sparkles to help.
3. **Pip arrives** and asks you to wake the old **Wonderbrush**. Take it from the desk (**E**). Sparkles point the way; after 20 seconds it sparkles again, and at 35 seconds Pip gives a hint. Picking it up saves a checkpoint. Then walk to the glowing **door** (**E**).
4. **Hush Hollow:**
   * Hop over the little brook (**Space**). It is too narrow to fall into.
   * Then run along the striped mat and tap **Shift** to **Dash**. Dash is yours from the start.
   * Wake the two Wonderbrush stars, in any order. One is by the old well. The other is on the stone terrace: jump up the little step, then onto the terrace.
5. Follow the **berry stamps** on the road to the **berry arch** and walk through it (checkpoint).

## Chapter 1: The Ruby Orchard
1. **Berry trail:** follow the raised trail and jump the two gaps (**Space** while running). Falling into the soft berry juice just pops you back.
2. **Courtyard:** stand behind the giant gray berry and press **E** to push it onto the round berry picture. The bridge comes down. If stuck, press **H**: Pip sparkles the berry, then gives a clue, then shows an arrow.
3. **Ruby's rescue** (three stages, saved after each):
   * **Bouncing Berry Parade:** white circles with a berry show where a berry will land. Step out of the circles. After a clean round, the crystal floats down; tap it with **E**. Getting bounced is fine; the parade slows down after a few bounces.
   * **Mirror Berry Puzzle:** each mirror has a big arrow. Press **E** at a mirror to turn it. The light leaves a mirror the way its arrow points. Guide the light to the crystal. **H** gives hints, and the third hint turns a mirror for you.
   * **Friendly Dragon Dance:** Ruby chants "One… Two… BIGGEST wiggle!" and stomps (watch the 1-2-3 footprints). Hop onto a berry platform before the biggest wiggle. When the crystal appears above your platform, **hold E** to sweep-paint it. Missed it? She dances again.
4. **Red returns!** Ruby gives you **Flame Burst** (**F**). Its practice courtyard is **not built yet**, so F does nothing in this beta.
5. **Dash test** (uses the free Dash): go through the open hedge east of the clearing. On the red berry pads, **jump, then tap Shift** in the air to whoosh across. In Helper mode (or after a few tries) Pip adds the dash for you.
6. **Stickers (optional):** one is hidden behind a big berry bush near the start. One is on top of the swaying jam jars on the west side. One is on the last dash pad.

## Helpful settings (Esc → Settings)
* **Helper mode:** bigger reach, slower patterns, longer chances, auto-dash.
* **Read aloud** and **Sound captions**.
* **Text size**.
* **Reduced motion:** gentle crossfades instead of sweeping waves.
* **Camera helper** and **Camera speed**.
