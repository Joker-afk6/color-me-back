# BONUS FINALE SCRIPT - PRISM PALACE

**This is Chapter 4 / the conclusion, provided in addition to the requested Chapters 1-3.**  
**Target playtime:** 12-18 minutes | **Color required:** All three primary Guardians restored.  
**Core design principle:** The final conflict is resolved through creativity and kindness, not violence.

## Scene 4.1 - A palace of unfinished drawings
**CAM:** Gray palace halls hold floating frames filled with half-finished worlds. The Fade, a small shadowy figure with oversized expressive eyes, sits near a paint-covered easel.  
**FADE:** "Stop! Please don't look at my picture. I haven't finished it."  
**PIP:** "You made the colors disappear?"  
**FADE:** "I tried to paint something beautiful. But the red looked wrong... and the blue looked wrong... so I hid them all."  
**EXPLORER:** "Maybe it doesn't have to be perfect. We can paint together."  
**FADE:** "You would help me? Even if I make a mistake?"  
**EXPLORER:** "Especially then."

## Scene 4.2 - The six prism stations
Six reachable stations around an open atrium are labeled with text and shape icons: RED/apple, BLUE/wave, YELLOW/sun, PURPLE/spiral, ORANGE/spring, GREEN/leaf.  
**GAMEPLAY:** Activate three primary stations using rescued colors. Open the palette to create any missing secondary colors explicitly, then activate the remaining stations. All routes are broad and accessible without timed jumping.  
**FADE (after two):** "You're not scared when the paint goes outside the line?"  
**PIP:** "That's how some of the best surprises happen!"  
**FADE (after four):** "I think I want to try again."  
**EXPLORER:** "You can choose the first brushstroke."

## Scene 4.3 - Your very own sky
**ACTIVITY:** Player selects among six color swatches and three large brush patterns to place trails across a magical sky canvas. Allow simple taps or drag painting. No score, no judge, no wrong choices.  
**FADE:** "I made... this!"  
**EXPLORER:** "It's yours!"  
**PIP:** "Every color has a place - even gray, black and white!"  
**CAM:** Pull back to the restored Hush Hollow, Orchard, Bay, and Skylands, then to the child's colorful outfit.  
**ENDING:** "You did it! You found your colors!" / "We made them together."

## Postgame unlock: Rainbow Playground
An optional free-play village with safe decorating zones, creature stickers, paintable houses and a scrapbook. All colors unlocked. Free play is offline and saves locally. Do not force player-created drawings to be shared or uploaded.

## Completion requirements
- All six stations have shape and text labels, not color-only identifiers.
- When a primary is missing, entry clearly explains which Guardian remains; no softlock.
- Fade character is non-scary; there is no HP bar or destructive combat.
- The final painting is genuinely player-driven. No preset answer is graded.
- Save/continue and replay controls work safely.
